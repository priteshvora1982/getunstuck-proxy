Short version: GetUnstuck does not read, store, or transmit your conversation content. Ever. Your words stay in your browser.

What GetUnstuck Does

GetUnstuck is a Chrome extension that detects analysis paralysis patterns in AI conversations on platforms including ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity. When a pattern is detected, it shows a brief intervention inside your conversation.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not read, store, or transmit the content of your conversations
  • We do not require account creation or login
  • We do not track your browsing history or activity
  • We do not use cookies, advertising trackers, or analytics scripts
  • We do not sell data to any third parties
  • We do not know who you are

What Stays in Your Browser

The extension uses Chrome's local storage to store:

  • A count of how many times the intervention has appeared this week
  • An estimated API usage cost for the current week
  • Whether an intervention has already been shown in the current session
  • If you click "Nope" to flag an incorrect detection, that signal is stored locally

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly send it.

The Proxy Server

For subtle conversation patterns that aren't caught by local rules, the extension sends a request to our proxy server for semantic analysis. This request contains:

  • The platform hostname (e.g. "chatgpt.com" or "claude.ai")
  • A classification of the detected pattern type
  • A confidence score
  • Your Chrome extension ID (anonymous — not tied to your identity)

It does not contain any prompt text, conversation content, or personally identifiable information. The proxy server forwards the request to OpenAI's API using our API key and returns the result. Your words never touch our server.

The "Nope" Button

When you click "Nope" to flag an incorrect detection, we optionally store the following on our server to help improve accuracy:

  • Platform (e.g. "claude.ai")
  • Detection type (e.g. "expanding" or "looping")
  • Confidence score
  • Timestamp

No prompt text. No conversation content. No identity information.

Third-Party Services

The extension uses OpenAI's API (via our proxy server) for semantic analysis of conversation patterns. The data sent to OpenAI is limited to the metadata described above — never conversation content. OpenAI's privacy policy applies to data processed through their API: openai.com/privacy

Children's Privacy

GetUnstuck is not directed at children under the age of 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the extension after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
