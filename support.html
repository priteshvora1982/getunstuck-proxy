Common Questions
The balloon isn't appearing. What's wrong? ›

A few things to check:

  • The extension needs at least 3 prompts in a conversation before it starts analysing
  • Make sure the extension is enabled — click the GetUnstuck icon in your Chrome toolbar and check the toggle is on
  • Try refreshing the page after installing
  • If you've already seen the balloon in this session, it won't show again until you start a new conversation
The balloon fired but I wasn't spiralling. How do I give feedback? ›
Click the "Nope" button on the balloon before dismissing it. This logs the false positive and helps us improve detection accuracy. If it keeps firing incorrectly on the same type of conversation, email us with details.
Does GetUnstuck read my conversations? ›
No. GetUnstuck detects patterns in your conversation — things like how many new topics are being introduced, whether the same concern keeps resurfacing, whether work is being described as done but not shipped. It does not read, store, or transmit the content of your messages. See the full privacy policy.
Which platforms does it work on? ›
GetUnstuck currently works on: ChatGPT (chatgpt.com), Claude (claude.ai), Gemini (gemini.google.com), Perplexity (perplexity.ai), Notion, Linear, and Google Docs, Sheets, and Slides. More platforms coming based on beta feedback.
Is it free? ›
Yes — completely free during the beta period. We'll announce any pricing changes well in advance.
How do I uninstall it? ›
Go to chrome://extensions in your browser, find GetUnstuck, and click Remove. All locally stored data is deleted when you uninstall.
I'm a developer — where's the source code? ›
The proxy server is open source. You can find it at github.com/priteshvora/getunstuck-proxy. The extension source is not currently public but we're considering it.
Known Issues in Beta
Fires on debugging conversations
If you're troubleshooting a technical problem across multiple messages and uploading logs, the extension may classify this as a looping pattern. Click Nope to dismiss. Fix coming in a future version.
May miss very subtle paralysis patterns
The extension requires at least 3 prompts and clear signals. Paralysis that develops slowly across many messages may not always be detected.
Only shows once per session
The balloon appears once per conversation session. If you dismiss it and continue spiralling, it won't reappear until you start a new conversation.
